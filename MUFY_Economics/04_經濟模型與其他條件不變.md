## 1. 什麼是模型？(What is a model?)

### 白話解釋
**模型 = 把複雜的真實世界「簡化」成一個比較好懂的版本，用來理解和預測。**

### 生活例子：地鐵路線圖
深圳地鐵的路線圖**不是**真實地圖：它不畫出每條街、每棟樓，站和站之間的距離也不準。但它把「哪條線、在哪裡轉車」講得非常清楚，讓你**能用它來做決定**。
經濟模型也一樣：故意省略很多細節，只留下最重要的關係。

### 課程的英文定義（請背下來；來自課本 `Chapter_1.3`，官方定義表沒有收錄 *model* 這個詞）
> A **model** is a simplified description of the real world, used to understand and predict the relationship between variables. We can use a model to estimate what will happen to one thing when we change another thing.

- **變數 (variable)**：會變動的東西，例如價格、數量、收入。
- 模型的用途：**當一個東西改變時，估計另一個東西會怎樣**。

### 經濟模型的四種形式
> An **economic model** can be a verbal description, a numerical table, a graph, or a mathematical equation used to describe what is expected to happen in reality.

| 形式 | 例子 |
|---|---|
| 文字描述 (verbal description) | 「價格越高，人們買得越少」 |
| 數字表格 (numerical table) | 家具工廠的生產可能性表（`05` 課） |
| 圖 (graph) | 生產可能性曲線 PPF（`05` 課）、需求供給圖（`07` 課） |
| 數學方程式 (mathematical equation) | 例如 Qd = 100 − 2P |

## 2. 為什麼模型不完美？(Why aren't models perfect reflections of reality?)

### 課程原文
> Economics is a social science that involves human beings and their behaviours, so the models can never be perfect. We can't predict how humans will react to changes in their lives. Models can help us guess or estimate what is likely to happen.

### 白話
經濟學研究的是**人**，人的行為很難百分之百預測（有人漲價也照買、有人突然改變喜好）。所以模型只能**估計**「大概會發生什麼」，不能保證。

---

## 3. 假設 (Assumptions)

### 白話解釋
**假設 = 為了讓模型簡單，我們先「規定」一些條件成立。**

### 課程原文
> Economic models usually contain **assumptions**. We must make assumptions to simplify the model, as real life is much more complex.

### 課本例子：PPF 的假設
> When analysing production possibilities for a firm using the Production Possibilities Frontier (PPF) model, we assume there are only two possible products to produce. This allows us to map the possibilities on the x- and y-axes easily. For a firm that can produce more products, it would be harder to map these possibilities, as we would need more than two axes!

真實的公司會生產很多種產品，但如果有 5 種產品，就需要 5 條軸，根本畫不出來。所以我們**假設只有兩種產品**，一條 x 軸、一條 y 軸就夠了。（課本的 Sophie's Sweets 例子也是這樣：假設只做杯子蛋糕 cupcakes 和鬆餅 pancakes 兩種。）

## 4. 其他條件不變 (Ceteris paribus)

### 白話解釋
**ceteris paribus**（拉丁文；英語裡常讀成 "SET-er-is PA-ri-bus" 或 "KAY-ter-is PA-ri-bus"，兩種都有人用）= **「其他條件都不變」**。
意思是：我們研究「A 改變對 B 的影響」時，**只讓 A 改變，其他所有東西都保持原樣**。

### 生活例子：喝咖啡能不能幫你讀書？
你想知道「喝咖啡」會不會讓你讀書效率變高。
- ❌ 錯誤做法：週一沒喝咖啡、睡 5 小時、在吵鬧的宿舍讀；週二喝咖啡、睡 8 小時、在安靜的圖書館讀。——週二效率高，到底是因為咖啡、睡眠還是環境？**不知道！**
- ✅ 正確做法：兩天都睡 8 小時、都在圖書館，**只改變「有沒有喝咖啡」**。這就是 ceteris paribus。

### 官方英文定義（請背下來）
> **✅ 官方定義**　**Ceteris paribus**: "A Latin phrase meaning ‘all other things being equal’."

課本 (`Chapter_1.3`) 的說法（*remain unchanged* 和官方的 *being equal* 意思一樣，當作補充）：
> The Latin phrase '**ceteris paribus**' is very important in Economics. This phrase means **'all other things remain unchanged'**. When trying to predict the effects of a change in a variable, it is essential to change only one variable at a time.

### 為什麼需要它？
> In reality, it is challenging to predict the changes that will occur in a market or an economy, as multiple variables often change simultaneously. However, the models used in Economics assume that only one variable changes at a time. This is the only way we can reasonably make predictions of the impact a changing factor has on another factor.

### 課本例子
- PPF 模型：我們假設**資源的數量保持不變**（resources remain constant）。
- 題目常見問法（課本舉的兩個個體經濟學例子）：
  - *What will happen to the price of bananas if there is a decrease in market supply, **ceteris paribus**?*（香蕉供給減少時，價格會怎樣？其他條件不變。）
  - ***Ceteris paribus**, what will happen to the quantity demanded of Product A when there is a price decrease in a substitute Product B?*（替代品 B 降價時，A 的需求量會怎樣？）

👉 這兩題你在 `07` 課學完需求和供給後就會答了。看到題目寫 *ceteris paribus*，意思就是「**只考慮題目說的那一個變化，其他都別管**」。

## 5. 課本測驗：Economic Model Quiz（`Chapter_1.3`）完整解答

**Question 1.** What is an economic model used for?
a. Demonstrating the way in which economic fashions should be worn
b. Solving the basic economic problem
c. Demonstrating examples of relative scarcity
d. Understanding and predicting the relationships between variables

**Question 2.** True or false? Economic models can predict effects perfectly, because human behaviour is easy to predict.

**Question 3.** When using models, we make assumptions, in order to
a. make the model more interesting.
b. simplify the model, so it is easier to understand.
c. enable the model to be more complicated.
d. make the model more reflective of real life.

**Question 4.** The phrase 'ceteris paribus' means
a. all other variables remain unchanged.
b. cause and effect can be reversed.
c. there may be more than one answer.
d. other variables are unpredictable.

**Question 5.** Which of the following is a good example of using 'ceteris paribus' in an experiment?
a. We want to know how many cupcakes can be produced by a firm. We record production on two different days. One day there are three workers; the other day there are five.
b. We want to know which country is able to produce the most goods and services per person. We compare total production of the entire country for China and Australia.
c. We want to know the effect of a change in the weather on the quantity of ice creams sold. We keep the price of ice creams constant and record the quantity sold on a hot day and a cold day.
d. We want to know which student has best achieved the learning outcomes for Unit 1 Economics. We give one student an exam that contains multiple choice questions; the other student must write an essay to demonstrate knowledge.

<details>
<summary>👉 答案與解析</summary>

**Q1：d**——定義原文：*used to understand and predict the relationship between variables*。（b 不對：模型幫助我們分析稀缺下的選擇，但不能「解決」稀缺。）

**Q2：False**——課本說 *"the models can never be perfect. We can't predict how humans will react"*。

**Q3：b**——*"We must make assumptions to simplify the model"*。d 剛好相反：假設讓模型**更簡單**，而不是更像真實世界。

**Q4：a**——課本測驗的選項寫 *all other variables remain unchanged*；✅ 官方定義的說法是 *all other things being equal*，意思一樣。

**Q5：c**——只改變「天氣」，並且把「價格」固定不變，這樣銷量的差異才能歸因於天氣。
- a：想知道公司能生產多少杯子蛋糕，但兩天的工人數量不同（3 人 vs 5 人），條件沒有保持一致，兩天的產量沒辦法拿來回答這個問題。
- b：比較「每人」產量卻用「全國總產量」比較，而且兩國人口完全不同，很多條件都不一樣。
- d：兩個學生考的形式不同（選擇題 vs 論文），不是只有一個變數在變。

</details>

## 常見錯誤

1. ❌ 以為假設是讓模型「更真實」。✅ 假設是讓模型**更簡單**；代價是沒那麼真實。
2. ❌ 寫 ceteris paribus 的意思時只寫「不變」。✅ 照官方定義寫完整：***A Latin phrase meaning ‘all other things being equal’.***（課本的 *all other things remain unchanged* 意思一樣）
3. ❌ 以為模型錯了就沒用。✅ 模型不完美，但能幫我們**估計和預測**——就像地鐵圖不準確但很好用。
4. ❌ 一題裡同時考慮好幾個變化。✅ 題目寫 *ceteris paribus*，就**只分析題目給的那一個變化**。

## 自我檢查

**Q1.** Define a model.（用英文）

**Q2.** 為什麼 PPF 模型假設只有兩種產品？

**Q3.** 你想研究「下雨會不會讓外賣訂單變多」。用 ceteris paribus 的想法，你應該把哪些東西保持不變？（說出兩個）

**Q4.** 判斷對錯：「因為經濟模型做了假設，所以它沒辦法告訴我們任何真實世界的事。」

<details>
<summary>👉 點我看答案</summary>

**A1.** *A model is a simplified description of the real world, used to understand and predict the relationship between variables.*

**A2.** 為了**簡化**模型：兩種產品剛好可以放在 x 軸和 y 軸上畫出來；產品更多就需要更多條軸，很難畫也很難看懂。

**A3.** 例如：外賣的**價格/運費**、**平台的優惠活動**、**星期幾（平日或週末）**、**時段（午餐或晚餐）**、**天氣以外的特殊事件（例如考試週）**——任選兩個合理的都可以。只讓「有沒有下雨」改變。

**A4.** **錯**。模型不完美，但它能幫助我們**理解和預測**變數之間的關係，估計「很可能」會發生什麼。

</details>
