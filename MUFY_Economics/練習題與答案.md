## B. 簡答題 (Short-answer questions)

> 答題結構建議：**定義 → 解釋（因果）→ 例子（或圖）→ 回扣題目**。詳見 [`考試技巧.md`](考試技巧.md)。

**B1.** Define opportunity cost. Use an example of a student's use of time to explain your answer. *(3 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*Opportunity cost: The value of the next best alternative is lost when making a decision. For example, a student has three free hours on Sunday (a limited resource). Her first choice is to study for her Economics task and her next best alternative is to work a part-time shift. The opportunity cost of studying is therefore the income (and experience) she could have earned from the shift. It is only the next best alternative, not the sum of all alternatives.*

**得分點**：(1) 正確定義——照 ✅ 官方定義原文寫（value、next best alternative、is lost when making a decision）(2) 指出有限的資源和選了什麼 (3) 指出**次佳**選擇就是機會成本。（`03`）

</details>

**B2.** Distinguish between microeconomics and macroeconomics. Give one example of each. *(4 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*Microeconomics is the branch of economics that studies decision making by individuals, households and firms in a specific industry sector or market. An example is a bubble tea shop deciding what price to charge. Macroeconomics is the branch of Economics that studies decision making for the economy as a whole. It looks at issues such as national output, employment and inflation. An example is a rise in a country's unemployment rate. In short, macroeconomics looks at the 'big picture', while microeconomics studies the small parts (people, firms, markets) that make up the economy.*

（兩個定義句是 ✅ 官方定義原文，只在前面加了 *Microeconomics is* / *Macroeconomics is*。`01`）

</details>

**B3.** Explain the four factors of production and the reward that each receives. *(4 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
- *Land (the land resource) is natural resources from the earth, used to produce goods and services, such as farmland, oil and fish. Its reward is **rent**.*
- *Labour (the labour resource) is a human input to the production process; workers used to produce goods and services, such as teachers and doctors. Its reward is **wages or salaries**.*
- *Capital (the capital resource) is a good used to produce other goods and services, such as machines and factories. Its reward is **interest**.*
- *Enterprise (entrepreneurship) is the creative ability of individuals to seek profits by combining resources to produce goods and services. Entrepreneurs also take the risk of making a loss. Its reward is **profit** (or a loss if the business is unsuccessful).*

（每一句的定義部分都是 ✅ 官方定義原文；例子和報酬來自 `02` 課。）

</details>

**B4.** Explain why economists use models and assumptions. In your answer, explain the meaning of *ceteris paribus*. *(4 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*A model is a simplified description of the real world used to understand and predict the relationship between variables. Real life is very complex and human behaviour is hard to predict, so economists make assumptions to simplify the model. For example, the PPF model assumes only two products, fixed resources and constant technology. An important assumption is ceteris paribus, a Latin phrase meaning ‘all other things being equal’. By changing only one variable at a time, economists can predict the effect of that one change on another variable.*

（*model* 的定義來自課本；*ceteris paribus* 用的是 ✅ 官方定義原文。`04`）

</details>

**B5.** Explain the law of increasing opportunity costs and why it causes the PPF to be bowed outward. *(3 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*The law of increasing opportunity costs states that as more of one product is produced, the opportunity cost of producing each extra unit rises. This is because resources are not equally suited to producing all products. When an economy shifts production from agricultural products to university degrees, it moves the best-suited resources first and the worst-suited resources (such as farmers) last. Since the worst-suited resources are the ones that are best at producing the other product, more and more agricultural output is given up for each extra degree. This makes the PPF bowed outward (concave to the origin).*

（`06`）

</details>

**B6.** Compare how a market economy and a planned (command) economy answer the question 'what to produce?'. *(4 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*A market economy is an economic system whereby the interactions of buyers and sellers determine what should be produced, how it should be produced and how distributed. Decisions are decentralised: what to produce is determined by consumer demand and the price mechanism. When consumers want more of a product, its price and profitability rise, so privately owned firms produce more of it (consumer sovereignty and the profit motive). A planned economy is an economic system whereby the government, rather than the free market, determines what should be produced, how it should be produced and how distributed. Decisions are centralised: the government decides what goods and services will be produced, in what quantities, and sets their prices. A market economy usually responds faster to consumers' wants, while a planned economy can direct resources to government goals but may create shortages or surpluses because planners lack information.*

（兩個定義句是 ✅ 官方定義原文。`06b`）

</details>

**B7.** Explain the difference between an **increase in demand** and an **expansion of demand**. *(3 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*An expansion of demand is a movement along the demand curve caused by a fall in the product's own price: the quantity demanded rises, but the demand curve does not move. An increase in demand is a rightward shift of the whole demand curve caused by a change in a non-price determinant, such as higher income, a rise in the price of a substitute, or a change in tastes. After an increase in demand, more is demanded at every price.*

（`07` 第 4 節）

</details>

**B8.** Using the price mechanism, explain the effect of a rise in the price of milk on the market for bubble tea. *(4 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法（照價格機制的步驟）：**
*Milk is an input into bubble tea, so a rise in its price increases the costs of production. This causes a decrease in the supply of bubble tea, shown by a leftward shift of the supply curve. At the original equilibrium price there is now a shortage, which puts upward pressure on the price. As the price rises, demand contracts until the shortage is eliminated. The final result is a higher equilibrium price and a smaller equilibrium quantity of bubble tea traded, ceteris paribus.*

**圖**：畫供給減少（`07` 圖 4）。（`07`）

</details>

**B9.** Explain how the price mechanism allocates resources in a market economy. *(4 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*Resource allocation is the assignment of scarce resources towards production of goods and services. In a market economy, this is done by the price mechanism, a system whereby the forces of demand and supply determine the price in a market. Prices act as signals and incentives. If consumers want more of a product, demand increases, causing a shortage and a rise in its price. The higher price signals that consumers value the product more and increases the profit from producing it. This gives firms an incentive to move more land, labour and capital into producing it, and new firms may enter the market. At the same time, products with falling demand experience lower prices and profits, so resources move away from them. In this way, scarce resources are allocated to the uses that consumers value most, without a central planner.*

（開頭兩個定義是 ✅ 官方定義原文。`07` 第 10 節）

</details>

**B10.** The traditional economics viewpoint sees decision making as rational. Explain how the behavioural economics viewpoint challenges this, using one example. *(3 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*The traditional economics viewpoint is a view that economic decision making is rational, and based on accurate information, an analysis of costs and benefits, and self-interest. The behavioural economics viewpoint is a view that economic decision making is influenced by psychological, social, emotional and cultural factors. For example, because of loss aversion, people feel the pain of losing ¥10 more strongly than the pleasure of gaining ¥10, so they may avoid sensible risks. These behaviours are not random; they are predictable patterns.*

（兩個定義句是 ✅ 官方定義原文。`06c`）

</details>

## C. 計算與畫圖題 (Calculation and diagram questions)

**C1.** Lin has ¥60 per week to spend on movie tickets (¥30 each) and bubble tea (¥10 each).
(a) List all the combinations that use exactly ¥60.
(b) What is the opportunity cost of one movie ticket? Of one bubble tea?

(a)

| 電影票 | 奶茶 |

| 2 | 0 |
| 1 | 3 |
| 0 | 6 |

(b) 1 張電影票的機會成本 = 3 杯奶茶（¥30 ÷ ¥10）；1 杯奶茶的機會成本 = 1/3 張電影票。兩者互為倒數 ✅。（`03` 的 Alphonso 例子同樣做法）

</details>

**C2.** The table shows the production possibilities of a country.

| Point | A | B | C | D | E | F |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Machines (units) | 0 | 10 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 |
| Food (tonnes) | 200 | 190 | 165 | 125 | 70 | 0 |

(a) Calculate the opportunity cost of moving from point C to point D, in total and per machine.
(b) Compare the opportunity cost per machine of the **first** 10 machines and the **last** 10 machines. What does this tell you about the shape of the PPF?
(c) Is each of these combinations attainable and efficient, attainable but inefficient, or unattainable? (i) 30 machines and 140 tonnes of food; (ii) 20 machines and 120 tonnes of food; (iii) 40 machines and 70 tonnes of food.
(d) What is the opportunity cost of moving from combination (ii) to point C?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

(a) C → D：多 30 − 20 = **10 台機器**，放棄 165 − 125 = **40 噸糧食**。每台機器的機會成本 = 40 ÷ 10 = **4 噸糧食**。

(b) 前 10 台（A→B）：放棄 10 噸 → 每台 **1 噸**；最後 10 台（E→F）：放棄 70 噸 → 每台 **7 噸**。每一段：1、2.5、4、5.5、7 噸，越來越大 → **機會成本遞增** → PPF **向外凸**（concave to the origin）。

(c) (i) 30 台機器時最多 125 噸糧食，140 > 125 → **做不到 (unattainable)**，在曲線外。(ii) 20 台機器時最多 165 噸，120 < 165 → **做得到但沒效率 (inefficient)**，在曲線內。(iii) 就是 E 點 → **在曲線上，有效率**。

(d) **零**。從 (ii)（20 台、120 噸）到 C（20 台、165 噸）是把閒置資源用起來，糧食多了 45 噸，機器沒有減少，**不用放棄任何東西**。（`05`、`06`）

</details>

**C3.** A bakery can produce either 600 cupcakes or 200 cakes per day, or any combination on a straight-line PPF.
(a) What is the opportunity cost of one cake? Of one cupcake?
(b) Draw and label the PPF.
(c) Is producing 120 cakes and 300 cupcakes per day attainable? Is 100 cakes and 300 cupcakes on the PPF?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

(a) 1 個蛋糕 = 600 ÷ 200 = **3 個杯子蛋糕**；1 個杯子蛋糕 = 200 ÷ 600 = **1/3 個蛋糕**。直線 → 每一個都一樣（固定機會成本）。

(b) 標題（例如 *PPF for a bakery*）；縱軸 *Cupcakes per day*，端點 600；橫軸 *Cakes per day*，端點 200；兩端用**直線**連起來，標 *PPF*。

```text
Cupcakes per day
  ^
600 *
    |   *
    |       *
    |           *
300 |- - - - - - - -X (100 cakes, 300 cupcakes: on the PPF)
    |               :   *
    |               :       *
    |               :           *
  0 +---------------+---------------*----> Cakes per day
    0              100             200
```

(c) 生產 120 個蛋糕時，最多還能做 600 − 3 × 120 = **240** 個杯子蛋糕；300 > 240 → **做不到**（在線外）。100 個蛋糕時，最多 600 − 3 × 100 = **300** 個杯子蛋糕 → 剛好在 PPF **上**。（`06`）

</details>

**C4.** An economy's PPF meets the X-axis at 200 units of good X and the Y-axis at 120 units of good Y. The economy is producing at point P, where it produces 150 units of X and 80 units of Y.
(a) How many units of X are given up to produce the 80 units of Y?
(b) How many units of Y are given up to produce the 150 units of X?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

(a) 全部生產 X 最多 200；在 P 點只有 150 → 放棄 200 − 150 = **50 單位 X**。
(b) 全部生產 Y 最多 120；在 P 點只有 80 → 放棄 120 − 80 = **40 單位 Y**。
小心不要把 P 點**生產的數量**（150、80）當成放棄的數量。（`05` 的 AS 考古題同樣做法）

</details>

**C5.** The table shows the demand and supply schedules for Product Z.

| Price ($) | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quantity demanded | 100 | 80 | 60 | 40 | 20 |
| Quantity supplied | 20 | 40 | 60 | 80 | 100 |

(a) What are the equilibrium price and quantity?
(b) Calculate the surplus or shortage at a price of $6, and explain what happens to the price.
(c) Calculate the surplus or shortage at a price of $3, and explain what happens to the price.
(d) New firms enter the market, so the quantity supplied **increases by 40 units at every price**. Find the new equilibrium price and quantity, and explain the change using the price mechanism.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

(a) $4 時需求量 = 供給量 = 60 → **均衡價格 $4、均衡數量 60**。

(b) $6：供給 100、需求 20 → **過剩 80**。企業降價 → 供給收縮、需求擴張 → 價格**下降**到 $4。

(c) $3：需求 80、供給 40 → **短缺 40**。價格面臨上升壓力 → 供給擴張、需求收縮 → 價格**上升**到 $4。

(d) 新的供給量：$2 → 60、$3 → 80、$4 → 100、$5 → 120、$6 → 140。$3 時需求量 80 = 新供給量 80 → **新均衡：$3、80 單位**。
價格機制：賣家變多 → **供給增加**（右移）→ 在原價格 $4 出現**過剩** 100 − 60 = 40 → 價格面臨下降壓力 → 價格下降、**需求擴張** → 新均衡**價格較低 ($3)、數量較多 (80)**。（`07` 圖 3）

</details>

**C8.** The diagram in `Lesson_2` (page 5) shows a straight-line PPF passing through point A (60 units of Good B, 200 units of Good A) and point B (90 units of Good B, 160 units of Good A).
(a) Calculate the opportunity cost of one extra unit of Good B.
(b) Calculate the opportunity cost of one extra unit of Good A.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

(a) 多 90 − 60 = 30 單位 B，放棄 200 − 160 = 40 單位 A → 1 單位 B = 40 ÷ 30 ≈ **1.33 單位 A**。
(b) 1 單位 A = 30 ÷ 40 = **0.75 單位 B**。
（提醒：投影片方框裡寫的「90」和「30」和它自己的圖對不上；這裡依**圖上的數字**計算，詳見 `06` 課例題 2。）

</details>
