## A. 選擇題 (Multiple-choice questions)

### Study Area 2（Week 6–8）

**A1.** The price of a cup of bubble tea rises from $10 to $12 and the quantity demanded falls from 200 to 150 cups per day. The price elasticity of demand is:
(a) −0.8　(b) −1.25　(c) −2.5　(d) −0.4

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**(b)**。%ΔP = (12 − 10) ÷ 10 = **+20%**；%ΔQd = (150 − 200) ÷ 200 = **−25%**；PED = −25% ÷ 20% = **−1.25** → 絕對值 > 1，**富有彈性**。(a) 是把公式上下顛倒（20 ÷ 25）。（`08`）

</details>

**A2.** If the demand for a product is price inelastic, a decrease in its price will:
(a) increase total revenue　(b) decrease total revenue　(c) leave total revenue unchanged　(d) shift the demand curve to the right

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**(b)**。缺乏彈性 → 價格和總收益**同方向**：降價 → TR 下降（需求量增加的百分比比價格下降的百分比小）。(d) 錯：自己的價格改變只會**沿著**需求曲線移動。（`08`）

</details>

**A3.** Which product is likely to have the most price elastic demand?
(a) electricity　(b) insulin for a diabetic patient　(c) one particular brand of bottled water　(d) petrol in the short run

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**(c)**。某一個牌子的瓶裝水有**很多相近的替代品**（其他牌子），漲價客人就換別家。其他三個都是必需品、替代品少（短期）。（`08`）

</details>

**A4.** An excise tax of $2 per unit is imposed on sellers. Ceteris paribus, this will:
(a) shift the demand curve to the left by $2　(b) shift the supply curve upwards by $2　(c) shift the supply curve downwards by $2　(d) increase the equilibrium price by exactly $2 in all cases

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**(b)**。消費稅由**賣方**繳 → 成本上升 → **供給曲線往上（左）平行移動**，距離 = 稅額。(d) 錯：價格通常上漲**少於**稅額，要看彈性。（`09`）

</details>

**A5.** The burden of an excise tax falls mainly on consumers when:
(a) demand is price elastic　(b) demand is price inelastic　(c) the tax is collected from sellers　(d) supply is perfectly inelastic

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**(b)**。需求**缺乏彈性** → 消費者離不開這個產品 → 賣方可以把大部分的稅加到價格上。(c) 誰繳稅（法律上）不決定誰真正負擔；(d) 供給完全缺乏彈性時，稅**全部**由生產者負擔。（`09`）

</details>

**A6.** A factory's pollution harms people living downstream. Without government intervention:
(a) MSC < MPC and the market under-produces　(b) MSC > MPC and the market over-produces　(c) MSB > MPB and the market under-produces　(d) the market is allocatively efficient

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**(b)**。負的**生產**外部性：**MSC = MPC + 外部成本** → MSC 在 MPC 上方 → 市場數量 Qm 大於社會最適 Q\* → **過度生產**（資源過度分配）。（`10`）

</details>

**A7.** Vaccination is an example of:
(a) a negative production externality　(b) a positive consumption externality, so the market quantity is less than the socially optimal quantity　(c) a positive production externality, so the market quantity is greater than the socially optimal quantity　(d) a public good, because it is non-rival

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**(b)**。打疫苗（消費）讓**第三方**也比較不會被傳染（群體免疫）→ **MSB = MPB + 外部收益** → Qm < Q\* → 分配不足。(d) 錯：疫苗是排他、敵對的（一針只能給一個人打），不是公共財。（`10`）

</details>

**A8.** Street lighting is a public good because it is:
(a) provided by the government　(b) non-excludable and non-rival　(c) free of charge　(d) rival but non-excludable

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**(b)**。公共財的兩個特徵：**非排他性**（不能不讓不付錢的人用）和**非敵對性**（你用了不會讓別人少用）。「由政府提供」或「免費」不是定義。（`10`）

</details>

## B. 簡答題 (Short-answer questions)

> 答題結構：**定義 → 解釋（因果）→ 例子、數字或圖 → 回扣題目**（D → E → E → L，見 [`考試技巧.md`](考試技巧.md) 第 5 節和 [`16`](16_小組論文與技能應用任務準備.md) B4）。

**B1.** Explain the difference between a positive consumption externality and a negative production externality. Use an example of each. *(4 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*A positive consumption externality occurs when the consumption of a product creates benefits for third parties. For example, when a student is vaccinated against influenza, other people are less likely to catch the disease, so the marginal social benefit (MSB) is greater than the marginal private benefit (MPB) and the market under-allocates resources to vaccinations. A negative production externality occurs when the production of a product imposes costs on third parties. For example, a factory that pollutes a river imposes costs on people downstream, so the marginal social cost (MSC) is greater than the marginal private cost (MPC) and the market over-allocates resources to that product.*

**得分點**：(1) 正確說明正的消費外部性（第三方**受益**、在**消費**時）(2) 好例子 (3) 正確說明負的生產外部性（第三方**受害**、在**生產**時）(4) 好例子。加分：說出 MSB > MPB／MSC > MPC 和分配不足／過度分配。（`10`）

</details>

**B2.** Using a demand and supply diagram, explain the effect of an excise tax on the equilibrium price and quantity in a market. *(4 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**圖要有**：縱軸 Price、橫軸 Quantity；D、S0、S1 = S0 + tax（S1 在 S0 正上方、平行，距離 = 稅）；原均衡 P0、Q0；新均衡 P1、Q1；生產者拿到的價格 P1 − tax。

**參考寫法：**
*An excise tax is a compulsory financial contribution to the government, levied on sellers of a particular product. The tax increases sellers' costs, so supply decreases and the supply curve shifts upwards from S0 to S1 by the amount of the tax. At the original price P0 there is a shortage, which puts upward pressure on the price. As the price rises, demand contracts until a new equilibrium is reached at a higher price (P1) and a lower quantity (Q1). The price received by sellers falls to P1 minus the tax.*

**得分點**：(1) 官方定義或正確描述 (2) 供給曲線往上（左）移、距離 = 稅 (3) 價格機制的過程（短缺 → 價格上升 → 需求收縮）(4) 結論：價格↑、數量↓，並引用圖上的標籤。（`09`）

</details>

**B3.** Explain how the price elasticity of demand affects the share of an excise tax paid by consumers. *(3 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*If demand is price inelastic, consumers continue to buy similar quantities when the price rises, so sellers can pass most of the tax on to consumers through a higher price; consumers therefore pay most of the tax (for example, cigarettes). If demand is price elastic, consumers reduce their purchases a lot when the price rises, so sellers cannot raise the price by much and must absorb most of the tax themselves. In general, the more inelastic side of the market bears more of the tax.*

**得分點**：(1) 缺乏彈性 → 消費者負擔多，並解釋原因 (2) 富有彈性 → 生產者負擔多，並解釋原因 (3) 例子或一般規則。（`09`）

</details>

**B4.** Distinguish between explicit costs and implicit costs. Give an example of each for a small business. *(4 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*Explicit costs are expenses of a firm that have a clearly defined monetary amount and must be paid for, such as the rent a phone repair shop pays for its premises. Implicit costs are opportunity costs that arise when a firm uses its resources for one particular purpose rather than an alternative purpose, such as the salary the owner gives up by working in her own shop instead of working for another company. Explicit costs are recorded by accountants, while implicit costs are not paid to anyone, but both are included when calculating economic profit.*

**得分點**：(1) 外顯成本的定義 (2) 例子 (3) 隱含成本的定義（機會成本）(4) 例子。（`11`）

</details>

**B5.** Define the law of diminishing returns and explain why it applies only in the short run. *(3 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*The law of diminishing returns is the principle that after some point, as variable resources are added to a production process involving fixed resources, the marginal product will start to decline. For example, as more workers are added to a kitchen with a fixed number of stoves, each extra worker adds less output because they must share the stoves. It applies only in the short run, because in the long run all resources are variable, so the firm can increase its stoves and kitchen space as well.*

**得分點**：(1) 官方定義 (2) 原因：固定資源不夠分（可用例子）(3) 短期至少有一種固定資源；長期所有資源都可以變。（`11`）

</details>

**B6.** Explain why the marginal cost curve cuts the average total cost curve at its minimum point. *(3 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*When the marginal cost of an extra unit is below average total cost, producing it pulls the average down, so ATC falls. When marginal cost is above average total cost, producing the extra unit pulls the average up, so ATC rises. Therefore ATC stops falling and starts rising exactly where MC = ATC, which is the minimum point of the ATC curve.*

**得分點**：(1) MC < ATC → ATC 下降 (2) MC > ATC → ATC 上升 (3) 所以 MC = ATC 在最低點。（`12`）

</details>

**B7.** Identify and explain two sources of economies of scale. *(4 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法（任選兩個）：**
- *Purchasing (bulk-buying) economies: a large firm buys raw materials in large quantities and receives discounts, so the cost per unit falls.*
- *Technical economies: a large firm can afford larger, more efficient machinery and production lines, which lowers the average cost of each unit.*
- *Specialisation: in a large firm, workers and managers can specialise in particular tasks and become more productive.*
- *Financial economies: large firms can borrow at lower interest rates.*
- *Marketing economies: advertising costs are spread over a larger output.*

**得分點**：每一個來源：(1) 說出名稱 (2) 解釋為什麼讓**長期平均成本下降**。（`12`）

</details>

**B8.** Describe three characteristics of an oligopoly. *(3 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*An oligopoly is a market structure with high barriers to entry, where a few large sellers have the majority of the market share and are mutually interdependent. First, a few large firms dominate the market (a high concentration ratio). Second, there are high barriers to entry, such as high start-up costs or economies of scale. Third, the firms are mutually interdependent: the actions of one firm, such as a price cut, affect its rivals, so each firm must consider how its rivals will react.*

**得分點**：每個特徵 1 分：少數大企業佔大部分市場、進入障礙高、互相依賴（也可以寫：非價格競爭多、可能勾結）。（`13`、`15`）

</details>

**B9.** Explain why firms in perfect competition earn only normal profit in the long run. *(4 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*In perfect competition it is easy to enter and exit the market. If firms are earning supernormal profit, new firms are attracted into the industry. This increases market supply, so the market price falls, and it keeps falling until price equals minimum average total cost and economic profit is zero. If firms are making losses, some firms leave, supply decreases and the price rises until losses disappear. Therefore, in the long run, firms earn only normal profit (economic profit equals zero).*

**得分點**：(1) 沒有進入障礙／自由進出 (2) 超額利潤 → 進入 → 供給增加 → 價格下降 (3) 虧損 → 退出 → 價格上升 (4) 結果 P = ATC 最低點，經濟利潤為零。（`14`）

</details>

**B10.** Explain why a monopoly may be able to earn supernormal profit in the long run. *(3 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*A monopoly is the only seller of a unique product and is a price maker. It produces where MR = MC and charges the price shown on the demand curve, which can be above average total cost, so it earns supernormal profit. Because there are very high barriers to entry, such as patents, legal licences or large economies of scale, new firms cannot enter to compete away this profit, so the monopoly can keep it in the long run.*

**得分點**：(1) MR = MC、價格在需求曲線上、P > ATC (2) 進入障礙很高（舉一種）(3) 新企業進不來 → 利潤不會被競爭掉。（`14`）

</details>

**B11.** Evaluate perfect competition and monopoly in terms of allocative and productive efficiency. *(6 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*Allocative efficiency is the condition in which scarce resources are allocated towards the production of goods and services that best satisfy consumer needs and wants, which occurs where price equals marginal cost. Productive efficiency occurs when firms produce at the lowest possible average cost, the minimum point of ATC.*

*In the long run, a perfectly competitive firm produces where P = MC = minimum ATC, so it is both allocatively and productively efficient. A monopoly produces where MR = MC but charges a price on the demand curve that is above marginal cost (P > MC), so it produces less than the allocatively efficient output and creates a deadweight loss. It also usually does not produce at minimum ATC, so it is not productively efficient.*

*However, a monopoly may achieve large economies of scale, giving it lower average costs than many small competitive firms, and its supernormal profits may fund research and development, leading to dynamic efficiency. Overall, perfect competition is more efficient in a static sense, but whether monopoly is worse depends on the size of its economies of scale and whether its profits are used for innovation.*

**得分點**：(1) 兩種效率的定義（P = MC；ATC 最低點）(2) 完全競爭兩種效率都有 (3) 壟斷 P > MC、無謂損失 (4) 壟斷通常不在 ATC 最低點 (5) 壟斷可能的好處（規模經濟、動態效率）(6) 有條件的結論。（`15`）

</details>

**B12.** Using game theory, explain why collusion between oligopolists may be unstable. *(4 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**參考寫法：**
*Collusion is the act of working together to make decisions rather than acting independently, for example agreeing to keep prices high. Game theory shows that each firm has an incentive to cheat on the agreement. If its rival keeps the high price, a firm can earn more by secretly cutting its price and taking customers; if the rival cuts its price, the firm also does better by cutting its price. Cutting the price is therefore each firm's dominant strategy, so the agreement tends to break down and both firms end up with lower profits, as in the prisoner's dilemma. Collusion such as price fixing is also illegal in many countries, which makes it harder to enforce.*

**得分點**：(1) 勾結的定義 (2) 每家都有作弊（偷偷降價）的誘因 (3) 用優勢策略／囚犯困境說明 (4) 結果：協議瓦解（也可以加：違法、難以監督）。（`15`）

</details>

**C1.** A cinema raises its ticket price from $15 to $18. The number of tickets sold falls from 400 to 360 per day.
(a) Calculate the price elasticity of demand. (b) Is demand price elastic or inelastic? (c) Calculate the change in total revenue. (d) Was the price rise a good decision for the cinema's revenue? *(5 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

(a) %ΔP = (18 − 15) ÷ 15 × 100% = **+20%**；%ΔQd = (360 − 400) ÷ 400 × 100% = **−10%**；PED = −10% ÷ 20% = **−0.5**
(b) 絕對值 0.5 < 1 → **缺乏彈性 (price inelastic)**
(c) 漲價前 TR = 15 × 400 = **$6,000**；漲價後 TR = 18 × 360 = **$6,480** → **增加 $480**
(d) **是**（對收益來說）：需求缺乏彈性時，價格和 TR 同方向，漲價讓 TR 增加。（還要看成本才知道利潤，但成本沒有給。）（`08`）

</details>

**C2.** The table shows the market for Product X.

| Price ($) | 4 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 10 | 12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quantity demanded | 80 | 70 | 65 | 60 | 50 | 40 |
| Quantity supplied | 20 | 40 | 50 | 60 | 80 | 100 |

(a) State the equilibrium price and quantity.
(b) The government imposes an excise tax of $3 per unit on sellers. Find the new price paid by consumers, the price received by sellers and the new quantity.
(c) Calculate the government's tax revenue and the share of the tax paid by consumers and by producers.
(d) Instead of the tax, the government pays sellers a subsidy of $3 per unit. Find the new price, the quantity and the cost of the subsidy. *(8 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

(a) Qd = Qs = 60 → **P0 = $8、Q0 = 60**
(b) 課稅後，消費者付 P 時，賣方只拿到 P − 3。試 **P = $10**：賣方拿到 $7，查表：$7 時供給量 = **50**；$10 時需求量 = **50** ✅ → **消費者付 $10、賣方拿到 $7、數量 50**
(c) 稅收 = 3 × 50 = **$150**。消費者負擔 = (10 − 8) × 50 = **$100**（2/3）；生產者負擔 = (8 − 7) × 50 = **$50**（1/3）。檢查：100 + 50 = 150 ✅。消費者負擔比較多 → 需求比供給**缺乏彈性**。

**C3.** In the market for Product Y, MPB = MSB = 50 − Q and MPC = 10 + Q (Q in thousand units, $ per unit). Production causes pollution with an external cost of $10 per unit.
(a) What type of externality is this? Write the equation for MSC.
(b) Calculate the market quantity and the socially optimal quantity.
(c) Calculate the welfare loss.
(d) Describe how a diagram would show this market, and explain how a corrective tax could fix the problem. *(8 marks)*

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

(a) 外部成本出現在**生產** → **負的生產外部性**；**MSC = MPC + 外部成本 = 20 + Q**
(b) 市場：MPB = MPC → 50 − Q = 10 + Q → **Qm = 20**（P = $30）。社會最適：MSB = MSC → 50 − Q = 20 + Q → **Q\* = 15**（P = $35）。→ 過度生產 5 千單位（**資源過度分配**）
(c) 在 Qm = 20：MSC = 40、MSB = 30，高 = 10。福利損失 = ½ × (20 − 15) × 10 = **25（千元）= $25,000**
(d) **圖**：縱軸 *Price, cost, benefit ($)*、橫軸 *Quantity*；**D = MPB = MSB**（向右下）、**S = MPC**、**MSC** 在 MPC 上方 $10；標出 Qm = 20（D 和 MPC 的交點）、Q\* = 15（D 和 MSC 的交點）；**福利損失三角形**在 15 到 20 之間、MSC 和 MSB 之間。
**矯正稅**：每單位課 **$10**（= 外部成本）→ 供給從 MPC 往上移到 MPC + 10 = MSC → 新的均衡在 **Q\* = 15** → 福利損失消失；政府稅收 = 10 × 15 = $150 千。（`10`）

</details>

**C10.** Two smartphone makers, Alpha and Beta, each decide whether to keep their price or cut their price. The payoffs (monthly profit, $ million; Alpha's payoff first) are:

| | Beta: keep price | Beta: cut price |
|---|---|---|

| **Alpha: cut price** | Alpha = 28, Beta = 8 | Alpha = 12, Beta = 12 |

(a) Does Alpha have a dominant strategy? Explain. (b) What is the likely outcome? (c) Explain why this is a prisoner's dilemma. (d) Why might the two firms find it difficult to agree to keep their prices? *(8 marks)*
